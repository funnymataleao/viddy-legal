The experience

Open it and start watching.

Guest access

No registration gate

Browse, search, follow channels on your Apple TV and watch without signing in.

Native playback

Built around AVKit

Familiar Apple TV controls for playback, captions, audio tracks, chapters and the queue.

Private

No tracking

No ads, no tracking and no analytics. Sign-in stays in the Apple Keychain on your Apple TV.

Viddy Plus

Optional, clearly presented.

Your subscriptions, history and playlists, up to 4K HDR, skipping sponsor segments and a personal Top Shelf. Monthly or yearly, with a free trial for eligible new subscribers.

You are charged only after confirming in Apple's purchase sheet (or when a free trial ends). The sheet shows the local price, period and renewal terms. Cancel any time in Apple TV Settings.
