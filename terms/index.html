Free use and Viddy Plus

Viddy can be used to browse, search and watch without signing in and without a purchase. Viddy Plus is an optional auto-renewable subscription, offered monthly and yearly, that adds signing in with your own account (subscriptions, history, Watch Later, playlists, likes and a personal Home), up to 4K and HDR playback, skipping sponsor segments with SponsorBlock, and a personal Top Shelf.

Eligible new subscribers may receive a free trial. Its length and the price that follows are shown on the purchase screen. Unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the trial ends, the subscription starts and is charged at the end of the trial.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase (or when a free trial ends). On Apple TV, manage or cancel in Settings → Profiles and Accounts → your profile → Subscriptions. You can restore purchases in Viddy Settings. Refunds are handled by Apple. Earlier one-time "lifetime" purchases remain honoured.
