Viddy Support

Quick answers for Apple TV, Viddy Plus, sign-in and playback.

Watching without an account

You can browse, search, follow channels on your Apple TV and watch as a guest. Signing in with your own account is part of Viddy Plus.

Plans unavailable

If plans do not load, check that Apple TV is online and open Settings → Viddy Plus again later. Free viewing keeps working meanwhile.

A video will not play

Check the internet connection, try another video and install the latest Viddy version. Some videos are unavailable because of region, age, account or service restrictions.

Purchases and subscriptions

Use Restore Purchases in Viddy Settings. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Apple TV Settings → Profiles and Accounts → your profile → Subscriptions.

Hiding comments and videos

Click and hold a comment to hide or report it, or a video card and choose Not Interested. These choices are stored on your Apple TV. Turn comments off in Settings → Playback → Show Comments.

Signing out

Settings → Account → Sign Out revokes Viddy's access to your Google account and deletes local history, follows and caches from this Apple TV. Purchases and preferences remain.

Contact the developer

For anything not covered above, email mail@denisefremov.com.

Include your Apple TV model, tvOS version, Viddy version, what you did and the exact message on screen.

Protect your account. Never send passwords, sign-in codes, cookies, payment-card details or other secrets. Apple handles App Store billing and refunds.

Source and acknowledgements

Viddy builds on open-source software. The source code and acknowledgements are available on GitHub. For private account or privacy matters, use email rather than a public issue.
