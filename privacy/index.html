Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Viddy is an independent Apple TV app for browsing and playing online video. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it goes, and the choices available to you. Viddy is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Google or YouTube.

Summary

  • Viddy does not track you. It uses no advertising identifiers, no cross-app tracking and no developer-operated analytics or crash-reporting service on Apple TV.
  • Network requests are needed to search, browse and play online content. The services receiving them can see your IP address and standard network metadata.
  • Signing in with your Google account is optional and is part of Viddy Plus. Sign-in credentials stay in the Apple Keychain on your Apple TV.
  • SponsorBlock lookups send only a short hashed prefix of the video identifier, never the identifier itself.
  • Apple processes all purchases. Viddy never receives your payment-card details.

Information handled by Viddy

Google account information (optional)

If you choose to sign in, Viddy may access your account display name, profile image, channel identity, subscriptions, playlists, liked videos and watch-history-related data. It sends only actions you request, such as subscribing to a channel, changing a playlist, rating a video or recording watch progress.

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored in the Apple Keychain on your Apple TV. Account display information and the cookies required for the signed-in session may also be stored locally so that the app stays signed in. This information is used only to provide the account features you see in the app. It is not sold, not used for advertising and not used to train AI models.

Viddy's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Your use of YouTube content through Viddy is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Playback and browsing

To browse and play videos, Viddy sends requests to Google/YouTube services and their content delivery networks. These requests can include video or channel identifiers, search terms you submit, locale and playback-capability information, authentication tokens when you are signed in, and normal network metadata.

SponsorBlock

When skipping sponsored segments is on (a Viddy Plus feature), Viddy asks the community service SponsorBlock (sponsor.ajay.app) for segment data. Viddy sends only the first four characters of a SHA-256 hash of the video identifier; the service returns data for many videos sharing that prefix, and Viddy picks the right one on your Apple TV. SponsorBlock can see your IP address and normal network metadata. No account information is sent.

Purchases

Apple processes Viddy Plus subscriptions through StoreKit. Viddy reads product information and Apple-signed transaction data, such as product identifier, entitlement and expiration status, free-trial status and whether a purchase is Family Shared, to unlock Plus features. Apple handles billing and payment details.

Information stored on your Apple TV

Viddy stores app settings, guest subscriptions (channels you follow without signing in), search and viewing history, playback positions, hidden videos and comments, cached images and metadata, and queue state on your Apple TV. A small amount of data, such as the videos shown in the Top Shelf, is kept in an App Group container shared only between Viddy and its own Top Shelf extension. None of this is sent to the developer.

Comments

Comments are loaded from YouTube. Hiding or reporting a comment in Viddy hides it on your Apple TV only; that choice is stored locally and is not sent to the developer. You can turn comments off in Settings → Playback.

Retention and deletion

Local data stays until you sign out or delete the app. Sign Out (Settings → Account) revokes Viddy's Google grant, removes the tokens from the Keychain and the session cookies, and deletes followed channels, search and watch history, playback positions, hidden items, feeds, queue and cached account data. Purchases and preferences remain.

You can manage information held by Google in your Google Account and revoke Viddy's access in your Google Account security settings. Apple keeps purchase records under Apple's policies. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Sharing

Viddy does not sell or share personal information for advertising. Information goes only where a feature you use requires it: Apple for purchases and platform services, Google/YouTube and their content delivery networks for catalog, account and playback, and SponsorBlock as described above.

Children

Viddy is not directed to children and does not knowingly build profiles of children. Apple and Google account rules and parental controls continue to apply; use Apple TV restrictions and your account's content controls.

Security

Viddy uses platform security features such as the Apple Keychain, HTTPS connections and StoreKit transaction verification. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

Changes

Material changes will be reflected here with a new date. If a change needs new consent, Viddy will ask before using account data for the new purpose.

Contact

The data controller for Viddy is Denis Efremov. Send privacy or data-rights requests to mail@denisefremov.com. General help is on the Viddy support page.

Never send passwords, sign-in codes, payment details or other secrets in a support request.
